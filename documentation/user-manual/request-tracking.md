# User Manual: Tracking Bookings & Printing Permits

This guide will help you track your reservations, upload payment receipts, and download your official permits.

## 1. Checking Your Booking Status
You can check the status of your requests at any time without needing to log in.

1. Go to the **Your Bookings** page.
2. In the search bar, enter either:
   * Your **Access Code** (e.g., `REQ-ABC123`)
   * The **Email Address** you used when making the booking.
3. Click **Search**.

### Understanding Your Booking Card
Once your booking appears, you will see a card with the following details:
*   **Status Badge:** Shows where your booking is in the process (e.g., Pending Approval, Awaiting Payment, Reserved).
*   **Details:** Shows your event title, purpose, schedule, and total fee.
*   **Actions:** 
    *   **Upload Receipt:** If your booking is "Awaiting Payment," you can upload a photo or PDF of your payment receipt here.
    *   **Cancel Request:** You can cancel your booking if it hasn't been finalized yet.

## 2. Viewing and Downloading Your Official Permit
Once your booking is finalized and paid, an **Official Receipt** button will appear on your booking card. Clicking this will open your Facility Use Permit.

### Why do I have multiple permits?
If you booked multiple venues (e.g., a Gym and a Classroom) or borrowed equipment (e.g., Sound System), the system generates a **separate permit for each item**. 

This is because different campus personnel manage different facilities. You will need to present the specific permit to the specific ground personnel for that venue or item.

### How to navigate the Permit Page
*   **The Sidebar (Left):** Lists all the permits generated for your event. Click on a venue or item to view its specific permit.
*   **The Permit Preview (Right):** Shows the official document with your event details and the digital signatures of the approving administrators. 
*   **The Receipt (Bottom Left):** Click "View Receipt" to expand a breakdown of the fees you paid. This is for your reference only.

### Downloading Your Permits
You have two ways to save your permits:
1.  **Download a Single Permit:** Hover over the venue/item in the left sidebar and click the **Download Icon** that appears.
2.  **Download All at Once:** Click the blue **Download All** button. The system will automatically capture every permit and bundle them into a single `.zip` file for you to download.

*Note: You can present the downloaded image on your phone, or print it out to present on the day of your event.*
